📖 Tutorial

1. Print out the AR target card.
2. Point your camera at the card to see your buddy.
3. Tap the floating + button to add a task.
4. Tap your 3D buddy to view your tasks hovering in AR.
5. When you feel overwhelmed, tap 🌸 Breathe for a guided exercise!
6. Complete tasks to earn Ankor Points and unlock outfits in the Customize menu!

⚓ Why Ankor?

Ankor Tasks is designed to keep you grounded. A traditional to-do list can feel overwhelming, but by combining interactive AR with physical repetition (like scanning your card), you build stronger memory retention and better habits.

We also prioritize mental health. When anxiety spikes, taking just a minute to breathe activates your parasympathetic nervous system, lowering stress and resetting your focus.
